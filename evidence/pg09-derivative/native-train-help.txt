Train a routed-particle slider on m-a-p/YuE2-3B. ConditionalPG09 trains the
public GAN-only recipe; winning_formulation() supplies adapter geometry.
--dummy does not download Hub weights.

options:

  --config_file CONFIG_FILE
  --audio_source AUDIO_SOURCE
                        Exact integration/audio-pg09 source checkout (default:
                        None)
  --core_source CORE_SOURCE
                        Exact shared PG09 core source checkout (default: None)
  --resume RESUME       Full training checkpoint; inference weights cannot
                        resume (default: None)
  --until UNTIL         Pause after this update, retaining the --steps recipe
                        horizon (default: None)
  --preset {bispf,bispf_optimized}
